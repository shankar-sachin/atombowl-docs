Account and settings

You can use most of Atom Bowl without an account. An account adds sync across devices, and unlocks Tutor and Generate.

Account

  • Sign in from the account page with Google or an email and password.
  • Use Forgot password on the sign-in form to reset by email.
  • Your stats, settings and Learn progress follow you between devices.

Settings

Settings apply instantly and are stored in your browser.

  • Moderator voice, speed, pitch and volume.
  • AtomCorrect on or off, and how strict it is.
  • Light or dark mode, font size, high contrast and animations.
  • Send missed questions to: Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini.
  • Look up answers with: Automatic, Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo or Brave.
