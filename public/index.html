Atom Bowl Docs

Atom Bowl is a free practice platform for the National Science Bowl (NSB). It runs in any modern browser with nothing to install.

Watch the tour

Practice

Tossups and bonuses read by a moderator, with NSB scoring.

Question bank

Search and filter 25,000+ real questions.

Learn

90+ short lessons across the NSB categories.

Tutor and Generate

Explain a missed question, or make new ones on a topic.

Buzzer rooms and clock

Run a live round with friends, and time it.

Account and settings

Sign in, sync, and tune the app.

Quick start

  1. Open Practice and pick your topics.
  2. Press Space to buzz while the moderator reads, then type your answer.
  3. After a miss, use Ask Tutor or send the question to Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini.

Atom Bowl is open source. The code is on GitHub.
