Troubleshooting

The page won't load or looks stale
Hard refresh with Ctrl Shift R (Cmd Shift R on Mac).
There is no voice
Pick another voice in Settings. Voices depend on your browser and operating system.
I can't sign in with Google
Allow pop-ups for the site and try again.
Tutor or Generate says it is having trouble
Check the status page. Failed requests do not use your credits.
Something else
Open an issue on GitHub.
Edit these pages on GitHub.
