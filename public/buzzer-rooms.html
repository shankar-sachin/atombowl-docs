Buzzer rooms and clock

Buzzer rooms let a moderator run a live round for friends or a team. Guests can play without an account.

Players

  1. Get the room code or invite link from your moderator and paste it into the join box.
  2. Pick your team, enter a display name and join.
  3. When the game starts, press Space or click the buzzer.
  4. You can see the timer, who buzzed first and who outbuzzed you.

Moderators

  1. Choose Create room, enter team names and your name, and pick official NSB rules or advanced settings.
  2. Share the join code with the Copy button.
  3. Open a tossup (choose the topic) or a bonus (choose the team).
  4. Score answers, then end the room when you are done.

Game clock

The game clock keeps team names, timers with sounds, scores and penalties for a round you run in person.
