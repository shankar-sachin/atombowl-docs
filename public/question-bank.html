Question bank

The question bank holds more than 25,000 tossups and bonuses. Search and filtering happen in your browser.

Banks

BankFormatSource
Scalazar (Set B)TextDOE nationals plus regional questions
Dynamazar (Set A)ImageOfficial DOE nationals questions

Text is closer to a real round, because everyone reads at a different speed, so Scalazar is listed first.

Using it

  • Search answers, question text, sets and rounds from the box at the top.
  • Narrow by bank, level, category and type (tossup or bonus, short answer or multiple choice).
  • Press Practice on any card to play that question in the practice engine.
