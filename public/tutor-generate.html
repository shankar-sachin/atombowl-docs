Tutor and Generate

Tutor and Generate are the two SI features. They are for signed-in players and use free daily credits. Everything else in Atom Bowl works without them.

Tutor

Tutor is a chat that explains a question, the answer and why. Open it from the floating button on any page, from /tutor, or with Ask Tutor after a practice question. The first reply is the fullest; follow-ups are quicker. Extra thinking spends 3 credits for a deeper answer.

Generate

Generate writes new tossups and bonuses for a topic and level you choose. Send them straight to practice to play them.

Credits and sign-in

  • You must be signed in. Email accounts need a verified address first.
  • Every account gets free credits each day, and your remaining credits show on the page.
  • If a request fails, your credits are refunded.
